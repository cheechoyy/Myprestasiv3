Three Pillars of Excellence

Every officer has unique potential. We believe excellence is measured not merely by workload, but through a combination of technical proficiency, operational fluidity, and human empathy.

Clinical Excellence

Digitally record every procedure and surgical log (CUSUM). We monitor your skill progression from the learning phase right up to mastery level.

Operational Efficiency

Track daily productivity, adaptation to new technologies, and innovative contributions. Every system improvement idea you propose is now recorded and valued.

Great doctors are exceptional listeners. MyPrestasi maps your Emotional Intelligence (EI), teamwork, and patient experience feedback to build a holistic personality profile.
